# 17. The watchlist

**Watchlist** in the sidebar keeps the authors, films and TV shows you are waiting for, including ones
that are only rumoured or announced. Each person has their own list.

## Adding something

Choose **Author (books)**, **Film** or **TV show**, type the name, and press **Watch**. It is looked up
straight away and then every six hours.

| Kind | What it finds |
|---|---|
| Author | Books from this year or last, and announced ones, that the library does not have (Open Library; Google Books as well when its key is saved). **Find it** searches for one in Discover. |
| Film | Films whose title contains every word you typed, coming ones first: announced, in cinemas from a date, out to watch at home from a date, out now, or already on the server. |
| TV show | The same for shows: starting on a date, running (with the next episode's date), ended, or on the server. |

Add a year to the name ("Dune 2026") to match only that year.

## Getting films and shows

- **Get it** asks for a match. One that is not out yet is added straight away and downloads once it is
  released (Radarr and Sonarr keep watching for it).
- **Get automatically** (or ticking it when you add the name) asks for every match that appears while it
  is still to come, without anyone pressing anything. It needs **Can request films and TV**. A match that
  is already out is never got by itself: press **Get it** for those.

## What is new

Anything found since you last opened the Watchlist is marked **New**, and Home shows a **News from your
watchlist** card until you open it. On the very first look, what is already there is not counted as news.

Tags followed for book suggestions are still on **Books → Discover**; authors added there and here are the
same list.
